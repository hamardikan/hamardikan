Hi, I'm Dika :3 I like cats, and I do software engineering in Jakarta.

I started as a chemistry kid: bronze at Indonesia's National Science Olympiad, top 30 out of about 35,000. At Universitas Indonesia I studied engineering physics and electrical engineering, but for a while my heart was in social science. I ran surveys, wrote papers ([one on how political ideology relates to students' social attitudes](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/mxpky_v1), N = 420), and in 2022–2023 chaired KSM Eka Prasetya UI, a student research club.

Then I drifted back to machines: control engineering, then AI on tiny chips, like fitting an [activity-recognition model onto an ESP32](https://github.com/hamardikan/har-esp32-optimization) in 0.18 MB for my thesis. A coding bootcamp came next (I finished top, 99%), and after it a few jobs at once: building web products for a Korean startup, analysing data for a national health study, and teaching chemistry.

Now I'm going deep into the hard side of systems, starting with DevOps and SRE as an apprentice at the Apple Developer Institute. Next, I want to work as an SRE or backend engineer somewhere with real scale, where traffic is counted in thousands of requests per second and a bad deploy is felt right away. Where I want to end up is inference engineering: making models run fast, cheap and reliably.

Along the way I build things that bug me. [Agent Awake](https://github.com/hmrdkn-labs/agent-awake) keeps my Mac awake while coding agents work, [LazySS](https://github.com/hmrdkn-labs/lazyss) gets me onto servers, and [Open Pajak](https://github.com/hmrdkn-labs/open-pajak) shows how Indonesian tax is actually calculated (50-odd stars and a few outside contributors). I also keep a free [DevOps course](https://devops.hamardikan.com), run a small Kubernetes cluster at home, and I'm building [a tiny village in Rust](https://github.com/hmrdkn-labs/mini-world) where every villager is a small on-device model. The rest of the time I'm losing my desk to Tabby, Karara and Mochi.
